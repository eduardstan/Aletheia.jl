# Grounding references

## File naming and licensing

PDF filenames use `authorYEAR-short-title.pdf`: the first author's family name in lowercase, the publication year, and a short lowercase hyphen-separated title. PDFs in this directory are redistributed only when an author or publisher makes an open-access/arXiv copy available. The shelf is a reading aid, not a replacement for the cited publications.

## Open-access shelf

* `kimmig2016-algebraic-model-counting.pdf` — Kimmig, Van den Broeck, and De Raedt, “Algebraic Model Counting”: the semiring-valued model-counting abstraction underlying probabilistic and weighted inference. [arXiv:1606.08326](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.08326).
* `darwiche2002-knowledge-compilation-map.pdf` — Darwiche and Marquis, “A Knowledge Compilation Map”: a precise map of tractable circuit representations and transformations. [arXiv:1106.1819](https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.1819).
* `manhaeve2018-deepproblog-neural-probabilistic-logic-programming.pdf` — Manhaeve et al., “DeepProbLog”: neural predicates combined with probabilistic logic programming. [arXiv:1805.10872](https://arxiv.org/abs/1805.10872).
* `li2023-scallop-language-for-neurosymbolic-programming.pdf` — Li et al., “Scallop”: differentiable, provenance-aware neurosymbolic programming. [arXiv:2304.04812](https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.04812).
* `xu2018-semantic-loss-functions.pdf` — Xu et al., “Semantic Loss Functions for Neuro-Symbolic Structured Prediction”: compiling logical constraints into a probabilistic training loss. [arXiv:1711.11157](https://arxiv.org/abs/1711.11157).
* `serafini2016-logic-tensor-networks.pdf` — Serafini and d’Avila Garcez, “Logic Tensor Networks”: tensor-based fuzzy grounding of first-order theories. [arXiv:1606.04422](https://arxiv.org/abs/1606.04422).
* `yan2020-neurasp-neural-networks-answer-set-programming.pdf` — Yang et al., “NeurASP”: neural atoms integrated with answer-set programming. [arXiv:2009.10256](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.10256).

The supplied campaign PDFs are normalized as `stan2026-symbols-and-neurons.pdf` and `riguzzi2023-foundations-of-probabilistic-logic-programming.pdf`.

## Existing grounding works

The original shelf also includes Blackburn, de Rijke, and Venema, *Modal Logic* (2001); Goranko, *Logic as a Tool* (2016); Schwarz, *Logic 2: Modal Logic* (2024, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0); Muggleton and De Raedt, “Inductive Logic Programming” (1994); Železný and Lavrač (eds.), *ILP 2008*; Cignoli, D’Ottaviano, and Mundici, *Algebraic Foundations of Many-Valued Reasoning* (2000); Hájek, *Metamathematics of Fuzzy Logic* (1998); and Galatos et al., *Residuated Lattices* (2007).

## Related unified frameworks
